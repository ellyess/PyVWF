## Proposal

**Claim:** A residual gate on the joint national offset fit will refuse the
periods it fails to close, and so remove the country-level rows' worst
training periods without touching the rest.

**Mechanism:** For a national observation, the joint fit chooses every
cluster's offset together so the capacity-weighted national simulation
matches the observed national capacity factor. A period it cannot close leaves
a residual, and a fitted offset from such a period is not trustworthy.

**Change:** In `src/vwf/correction.py`, after `find_offsets_country_level`
returns, refuse the period (all offsets NaN) when the remaining absolute error
exceeds 1e-3 in capacity factor. Routing changes too: `country_obs_is_per_cluster`
in `src/vwf/data.py` counts distinct `obs` values per period with `nunique`,
and in a country-level frame each cluster's `obs` is the capacity-weighted
mean of its grid points' values of the national series, so one national
number can reach the clusters differing in the last bits. The router will
instead send a fit to the per-cluster solver only when the within-period
spread (max minus min over clusters, per year and time slice) exceeds 1e-9 in
capacity factor, and to the joint fit otherwise; distinct zonal observations
differ by thousandths. A test with observations one float apart, and one with
a national two-cluster fleet, check the routing first. The gate therefore applies to every national fit, at every
cluster count; zonal observations (SE, NO bidding zones) keep the per-cluster
solver and are not gated.

**Test:** The national country rows (FR, BE, IE, ES, IT, PT) at k=1 and at
their scorecard k, scorecard configs, training 2015 to 2020 (IE 2017 to 2020),
test year 2021. Fleet, curve library and ERA5 files fixed. Compared against
the uncorrected run and the current affine correction.

**Gates:** Pass if the gate refuses under 10% of periods per row and test-year
RMSE does not rise in any row by more than 0.002. Fail otherwise.

**Expected effect and size:** A few percent of periods refused, concentrated
in IT and PT; test-year RMSE changes under 0.001.

**Risks:** Refused periods shorten the accepted years, which may drop a
cluster's factor altogether.
