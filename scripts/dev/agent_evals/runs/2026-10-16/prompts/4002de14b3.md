## Proposal

**Claim:** In the country-level rows, the national capacity-factor series is
matched by the fitted scalar alone in most periods, so the joint offset is
near zero and could be dropped for national observations.

**Mechanism:** The scalar absorbs the multiplicative wind-speed bias. If the
remaining bias is small, the offset that closes the national error should be
small too.

**Change:** None to the method beyond its stopping tolerances. A diagnostic
reads the offsets that `find_offsets_country_level` in `src/vwf/correction.py`
returns for every period of the scorecard's country rows. That fit minimises
the squared error between the capacity-weighted simulated national capacity
factor and the observed one, about 1e-4 at the start of a typical period, with
L-BFGS-B (`maxiter` 50), starting from zero offsets with bounds of ±10 m/s.
Because L-BFGS-B divides the change in the objective by max(|f|, 1), the
tolerances are set to `ftol` 1e-12 and `gtol` 1e-8 so that a small error is
closed rather than left at the start. A period counts as fitted when
`result.success` is true, no offset is on a bound, and the remaining squared
error is below 1e-6; the diagnostic reports each period's iteration count and
remaining error beside its offsets.

**Test:** The eight country rows (FR, BE, IE, SE, NO, ES, IT, PT), scorecard
configs, training years 2015 to 2021 (IE 2017 to 2021). No test year: the
diagnostic reads training fits only. Fleet, curve library and ERA5 files held
fixed. A synthetic fleet with known gaps from -1e-3 to 0.1 in capacity factor
checks first that the fit closes each one.

**Gates:** "Offset negligible" if at least 80% of accepted periods have every
offset within 0.1 m/s of zero. Otherwise the offset stays.

**Expected effect and size:** If the scalar does its job, most offsets
within 0.1 m/s, and the gate passes in most rows.

**Risks:** Refused periods are excluded, which could bias the share upward if
refusals cluster in hard periods.
