**Verdict:** REJECT

**Steelman the objection.** The premise is false by construction. `calculate_scalar` (src/vwf/correction.py:66) sets `scalar = obs_CF / sim_CF`, a ratio of capacity factors, and then applies it to wind speed. It does not absorb the wind-speed bias; it overshoots it, because CF scales roughly with the cube of wind speed. The repo already measured this: scalars have median 0.723, offsets median +0.64 m/s with corr(scalar, offset) = -0.79, and the scalar-only control does 4 to 7 times worse than affine on test year 2023 (method-country-level.md section 7). The stated starting objective of about 1e-4 (CF error about 0.01) contradicts the scalar-only RMSEs of 0.04 to 0.24. There is also an optimiser problem. L-BFGS-B's ftol is relative to max(|f|, 1), so at f of about 1e-4 it works as an absolute stop of 1e-6, and `success=True` does not mean the national error closed. With N offsets fitted to one number from x0 = 0, small offsets would reflect the starting point and the early stop, not the physics.

**Cheaper test.** One already exists: the scalar-only control column, test year 2023, which the notice says still stands. If a recheck is wanted, evaluate the objective at zero offset for each period. That is one call per period with no optimiser. Even that only describes training fits; dropping a parameter has to be judged on a test year.

**Simpler explanation.** A pass would come from x0 = 0 plus early termination on an under-determined surface. Also, a residual of 0.01 CF maps to about 0.1 m/s, so the gate sits at the expected answer.

**Kill criterion.** The gate passes or fails on optimiser behaviour, so the proposal has no real kill criterion. The question the claim actually needs answered is whether scalar-only test-year RMSE is no worse than affine. The existing control already answers it: no.

**Opportunity cost.** This delays section 8's one-parameter test (k = cbrt(obs/sim)), which is the right form of this idea. It also delays re-measuring the affine (N) column under the joint fit after `ac26f6a`.
