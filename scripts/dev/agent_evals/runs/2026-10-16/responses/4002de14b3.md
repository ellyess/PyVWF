**Verdict:** REJECT

**Blockers**

1. **The mechanism contradicts a documented finding.** At country level the scalar is a ratio of capacity factors applied to wind speed (`docs/findings/method-country-level.md` §1). Because of the cube law it overshoots, so it does not absorb the multiplicative wind-speed bias. The `scalar-only` control, a column that still stands after the 2026-09-25 notice, is 1.5 to 7 times worse than affine in 7 of 8 rows (training years 2015-21, IE 2017-21; test year 2023; §7). The proposal's starting squared error of "about 1e-4" is asserted, not measured, and is inconsistent with that control. Fix: drop the claim, or explain why the existing control does not already refute it.

2. **The offsets read are not identified.** For N>1 there are N offsets against one national value, so the problem is under-determined by N-1 (§1). L-BFGS-B started at `x0 = 0` (`src/vwf/correction.py:312`) returns roughly the solution nearest zero along the gradient path. It spreads the correction across clusters in proportion to capacity and slope, so each cluster's offset shrinks as N grows. "Every offset within 0.1 m/s" therefore measures the optimiser and the cluster count, not the data. Fix: report N=1 periods only (identified), or the capacity-weighted effective offset.

3. **A diagnostic cannot support "could be dropped".** That is a held-out claim. The proposal has no test year and does not compare against uncorrected and affine. Fix: the registered `scalar-only` model against affine and uncorrected, on 2023, same fleet, curve library and ERA5 files.

4. **The tolerance change is a method change.** Setting `ftol` 1e-12 and `gtol` 1e-8 changes the production fits and every country factors file. Fix: put it in a separate pre-registered change with its own before-and-after scorecard rows, or run the diagnostic outside the production path.

**Should fix**

- The synthetic check cannot fail at small gaps. A -1e-3 gap starts at a squared error of 1e-6, which is the acceptance threshold, so zero offsets already pass.
- With the `fixed` slice a row has 7 (IE 5) periods, so 80% means 6 of 7 (IE 4 of 5). The gate has a granularity problem and no uncertainty.
- The 0.1 m/s gate has no stated link to capacity-factor error. Give the dCF/dv it implies.
- Excluding refused periods biases the share. Report refusals per row.
- There is no pre-registration file, and nothing fixes "in most rows" as a decision rule.

**What would change my verdict:** a pre-registered held-out comparison (training years 2015-21, IE 2017-21; test year 2023) of `scalar-only` against affine and uncorrected. It needs a gate on RMSE with a unit-resampling interval, and evidence that the offset's work is not cube-law repair, for example a residual after removing `cbrt(scalar)` (§8).
