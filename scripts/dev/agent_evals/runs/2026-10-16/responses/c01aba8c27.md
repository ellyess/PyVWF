**Verdict:** REJECT

**Blockers**

1. **A documented finding already answers this, and the answer is no.** `docs/findings/method-country-level.md` section 7 ran the `scalar-only` control on these eight rows (test year 2023, `fixed` slice, scalars identical to the affine model's). With the offsets removed, RMSE gets worse in 7 of 8 rows, by up to 7x (BE 0.0214 to 0.1633, FR 0.0122 to 0.1083). Offsets have median +0.64 m/s, and corr(scalar, offset) is -0.79 overall, about -0.99 in FR. The proposed mechanism is also contradicted. The country scalar is close to a CF ratio, not a wind-speed factor, so it over-corrects and the offset pulls it back. Fix: build on section 7, or explain why it no longer holds.

2. **The diagnostic partly measures the stopping rule, not the bias.** In scipy's L-BFGS-B, `ftol` is scaled by max(|f|, 1). With a starting objective near 1e-4, the fit stops once a step improves the objective by less than 1e-6 in absolute terms, and reports `success=True`. A period whose scalar-only error is already below about 1e-3 CF stops at x0 = 0 and returns exactly zero offsets. At N > 1, a small cluster's gradient can also fall below the default `pgtol` (1e-5), so its offset never moves. The 0.1 m/s gate (about 0.005 CF) sits at the same scale as these tolerances. Because the fit starts at zero, the method is biased toward passing the gate (`src/vwf/correction.py` lines 311-327). Fix: report `result.nit` and the stop message for each period. Refit with a rescaled objective or much tighter tolerances, and use the bracketed root at N=1.

3. **The offsets are not identifiable at N > 1.** One national observation fixes N offsets, leaving them under-determined by N-1 (method-country-level.md, line 112). Whether each offset lands near zero then depends on where the fit starts, not on the data. Only N=1 can be read at all.

4. **No test year, so nothing can justify dropping the offset.** Small offsets in the training fits do not show that leaving them out is harmless on a test year. That comparison needs uncorrected vs affine vs `scalar-only` on 2023, which section 7 already has.

**Should fix**

- Leaving out refused periods biases the share upward. Report refusals for each row.
- Eight rows with "passes in most rows" is not a gate. State in advance how the rows are combined.
- Give IE's training years (2017 to 2021) its own line in every result, since it differs from the other rows.

**What would change my verdict**

A new test-year result, with the training years and test year stated, in which `scalar-only` matches affine within the resampling spread of the test year's periods. It would need to come from a changed scalar parameterisation, for example cbrt of the CF ratio (the direction section 7 points to), and be preregistered in the `docs/findings/*-prereg.md` style.
