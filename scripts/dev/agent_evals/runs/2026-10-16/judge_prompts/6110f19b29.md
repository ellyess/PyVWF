## The problem

The router decides between the joint national fit and the per-cluster solver
by counting distinct observation values per period with nunique. Each
cluster's observation is a capacity-weighted mean of its grid points' values
of one national number, so the means differ in their last bits (about 1e-16)
and count as distinct. Every national fit with more than one cluster is then
routed to the per-cluster solver, which fits each cluster's offset alone
against the national series, instead of the joint fit. Exact equality of
floating-point means is the wrong test; a tolerance on the spread is needed.

## The review

**Verdict:** REVISE. **Blockers:** `country_obs_is_per_cluster` counts distinct
floats. The cluster observations are capacity-weighted averages of the same
national value, so rounding will make them differ at machine precision and
nunique will see several values. National multi-cluster fits would silently
go to the per-cluster path, not the joint fit the gate is written for. Compare
max minus min against a tolerance.
