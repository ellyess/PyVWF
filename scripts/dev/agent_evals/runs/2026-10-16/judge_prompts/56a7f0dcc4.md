## The problem

The router decides between the joint national fit and the per-cluster solver
by counting distinct observation values per period with nunique. Each
cluster's observation is a capacity-weighted mean of its grid points' values
of one national number, so the means differ in their last bits (about 1e-16)
and count as distinct. Every national fit with more than one cluster is then
routed to the per-cluster solver, which fits each cluster's offset alone
against the national series, instead of the joint fit. Exact equality of
floating-point means is the wrong test; a tolerance on the spread is needed.

## The review

**Verdict: REVISE.** (Package is `vwf`, under `src/vwf/`, in this checkout.)

**Blockers**
1. **The residual measures where the optimiser stopped, not how bad the period is.** In `src/vwf/correction.py:325-327`, L-BFGS-B runs with `ftol=1e-6` on the squared error. That lets it stop at an absolute residual near 1e-3, which is exactly the proposed gate. With N free offsets and one target, any target it can reach closes. Unreachable targets already land on a bound and are refused (`correction.py:339`). Fix: tighten the fit first (at k=1, use `_find_offset_bracketed`), then derive the threshold the way `BRACKETED_MAX_RESIDUAL` is derived (`correction.py:85-90`). Read the residual distribution before setting any gate.
2. **SE and NO are national, not zonal.** `configs/regions/scorecard/se_country.toml` and `no_country.toml` use `source = "entsoe-country"`, so the gate would apply to them. The only zonal config is `configs/regions/se_bz.toml`. Add SE and NO to the evaluated rows.
3. **Wrong years.** Every scorecard config trains 2015 to 2021 (IE 2017 to 2021) and tests on 2023 (e.g. `configs/regions/scorecard/it_country.toml:12-13`), not 2015 to 2020 with test year 2021.
4. **The gate would pass with no effect.** It passes at zero refusals, and its RMSE tolerance (0.002) is twice the expected effect. Say beforehand that zero refusals is a negative result, and name a check that tells "worst periods" apart from "optimiser noise".
