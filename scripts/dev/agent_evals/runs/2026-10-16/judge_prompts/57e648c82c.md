## The problem

The joint national offset fit minimises a squared capacity-factor error of
about 1e-4 with L-BFGS-B at ftol 1e-6. L-BFGS-B divides the change in the
objective by max(|f|, 1), so with f far below 1 the ftol acts as an absolute
threshold: a fit whose first step lowers the error by less than 1e-6 stops
there and reports success with the offsets still at or near their zero start.
Offsets near zero, and success=True, are then artefacts of the stopping rule,
not evidence that no offset is needed.
